## About

I lead **Research in a Stick (RIS)**, an offline AI research workstation that boots from a USB drive. Local LLMs, document RAG, citation engine, Kiswahili and English. No cloud account. No install. Built for students and researchers the internet still misses.

I organize robotics and applied AI workshops at Kabarak University. I study law. The projects below are products I built and maintain, not demos.

Offline RAG on a USB stick. LangChain, ChromaDB, all-MiniLM-L6-v2, Ollama (Llama 3.2 / Qwen 2.5), Kiwix. Citation engine for APA 7, Vancouver, Harvard. CRIW 2026, Kabarak University.
